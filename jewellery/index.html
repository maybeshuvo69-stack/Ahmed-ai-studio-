    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    Is my order confirmed as soon as I send the form?

    No. Sending the form is an enquiry only. Your order is confirmed only when the shop contacts you to confirm the price, size, availability and delivery.

    Are the prices on this page final?

    No — every price here is a sample price (নমুনা মূল্য). The shop confirms the real price when they reply to your enquiry.

    Do I need to pay when I order?

    No payment is taken in this order form. Please ask the shop directly about payment options when they confirm your order.

    How do I know the right ring or bangle size?

    Mention your size in the order notes. The shop confirms the size and fit before your order is final.

    Do you deliver outside Dhaka?

    Delivery areas and charges are confirmed by the shop when they confirm your order.
