Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointments are taken via this booking request form, and also via the clinic’s Facebook Page, WhatsApp and phone. Please request a time first — the clinic will confirm it before your visit.

Is my booking confirmed as soon as I send the form?

No. Sending the form is a request only. Your appointment is confirmed only when the clinic contacts you to confirm the date and time.

Do I need to pay when I book?

No payment is taken in this booking form. Please ask the clinic directly about fees when they confirm your appointment.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring a note of any skin or hair products you currently use, and any questions you would like to ask. The clinic can tell you if anything else is needed when they confirm.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Contact the clinic as soon as you can via Facebook, WhatsApp or phone, and mention your name, service and requested date. The clinic will confirm the change.

Where is the center?

The center is in Dhanmondi, Dhaka, Bangladesh. The exact address, phone number and opening hours were not verified for this concept demo — please confirm them via the clinic’s Facebook Page before visiting.
