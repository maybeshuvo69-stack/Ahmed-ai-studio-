How admission works

From enquiry to first class, in three steps

  1. 1

    Send an enquiry

    Fill in the admission enquiry form below with the course you are interested in and your preferred batch.

  2. 2

    Talk to the coaching centre

    The centre confirms the course, batch timing and fee, and answers any questions before you join.

  3. 3

    Join a batch

    Visit the Farmgate campus — or the Mouchak branch — and start classes with your batch.

FAQs

Common questions

How do I apply for admission?

Send an admission enquiry using the form on this page, or contact the coaching centre through its Facebook Page. The centre will confirm the course, batch and fee before you join.

How much are the course fees?

Fees are on enquiry — they are not listed on this demo site. Ask about the course you are interested in and the coaching centre will confirm the fee.

Where are classes held?

Classes are held at the Farmgate campus in Dhaka. There is also a Mouchak branch. Street addresses are not listed on this demo — please ask the coaching centre for directions.

Are there model tests?

The sample programmes shown on this page include model tests, and the sample schedule has a Friday model-test slot. Confirm the current test routine with the coaching centre, as it can change by season.

Which batch should I choose?

The sample schedule shows morning, afternoon and evening batches. Choose the one that fits your day, and confirm the current batch timings with the coaching centre before joining.

Can I visit before joining?

Yes — you can visit the Farmgate campus or the Mouchak branch to ask about courses, batches and fees. Please confirm opening times with the coaching centre first, as they were not verified for this demo.
