Simple by design

How booking works

Today, tour sales are booked manually — customers call or message the agency’s Facebook Page, and the agency notes the booking by phone. This demo keeps that way — and adds a tour list and a booking-request form, so every request is written down in one place.

  1. 1

    Choose a tour

    Browse the packages, search by place or filter by type, and pick the dates that suit you.

  2. 2

    Send a booking request

    Use the booking form below — or call / message the agency’s Facebook Page, as before. Tell the agency how many people are travelling.

  3. 3

    The agency confirms

    The agency contacts you to confirm seats, dates, the final price and what is included. No payment is taken in this demo form, and a request is not a confirmed booking until the agency confirms it.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a tour with The Holidays Tours?

Use the booking form on this page, or call / message the agency’s Facebook Page — the way the agency already takes bookings. The agency confirms every booking before it is final.

Is my booking confirmed as soon as I send the form?

No. Sending the form is a request only. Your booking is confirmed only when the agency contacts you to confirm the seats, dates, price and what is included.

Do I need to pay when I send a booking request?

No payment is taken in this booking form. Please ask the agency directly about payment options and any advance payment when they confirm your booking.

Where do the tours depart from?

The tours on this page depart from Dhaka — Sayedabad, Dhaka. The exact departure point and time for your tour are confirmed by the agency when they confirm your booking.

What is included in the tour price?

What is included varies by tour. The sample prices on this page are per person and are samples only — the agency confirms the real price and what is included when they confirm your booking.

Where is the agency?
