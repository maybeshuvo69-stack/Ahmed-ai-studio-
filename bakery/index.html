Simple by design

How ordering works

Ordering is kept simple: browse the catalogue, add items to the cart, and send an order enquiry. The shop confirms every order before it is final.

  1. 1

    Browse the menu

    Search by item or filter by category — cakes, cupcakes, cookies or breads — and check the size note on each sample item.

  2. 2

    Add to cart & send an enquiry

    Add items to the cart, then use the order form below — or message the shop directly.

  3. 3

    The shop confirms

    The shop contacts you to confirm the price, size, availability and delivery. No payment is taken in this demo form.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I order from this bakery?

Use the order form on this page, or message the shop — the way the shop already takes orders. The shop confirms every order before it is final.

Is my order confirmed as soon as I send the form?

No. Sending the form is an enquiry only. Your order is confirmed only when the shop contacts you to confirm the price, size, availability and delivery.

Do I need to pay when I order?

No payment is taken in this order form. Please ask the shop directly about payment options when they confirm your order.

Do you deliver outside Dhaka?

The shop delivers all over Bangladesh, as it offers. Delivery details and charges are confirmed by the shop when they confirm your order.

Can I order a custom cake?

Yes — mention the flavour, size, message and date in the order notes, and the shop will confirm whether it can be made.
