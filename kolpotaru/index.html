Simple by design

How ordering works

Today, customers order by messaging the store’s Facebook Page. This demo keeps that way — and adds a catalogue and an order form, so nothing gets lost in the inbox.

  1. 1

    Browse the catalogue

    Search by style or filter by category, and check the size guide before you order.

  2. 2

    Send your order

    Use the order form below — or message the store’s Facebook Page, as before. Choose Messenger or WhatsApp for the reply.

  3. 3

    The store confirms

    The store contacts you to confirm the price, size, availability and delivery. No payment is taken in this demo form.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I order from Kolpotaru?

Use the order form on this page, or message the store’s Facebook Page, or order by Messenger / WhatsApp — the way the store already takes orders. The store confirms every order before it is final.

Is my order confirmed as soon as I send the form?

No. Sending the form is an enquiry only. Your order is confirmed only when the store contacts you to confirm the price, size, availability and delivery.

Do I need to pay when I order?

No payment is taken in this order form. Please ask the store directly about payment options when they confirm your order.

How do I choose the right size?

Check the size guide on this page. If you are between two sizes, mention it in the order notes — the store can advise on the fit for the item you choose.
