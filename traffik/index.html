Simple by design

How ordering works

Today, customers order by sending a photo to the store’s Facebook inbox. This demo keeps that way — and adds a catalogue and an order form, so nothing gets lost in the inbox.

  1. 1

    Browse the catalogue

    Search by style or filter by category, and check the size guide before you order.

  2. 2

    Send your order

    Use the order form below — or send the style photo to the store’s Facebook inbox, as before. Choose Messenger or a phone call for the reply.

  3. 3

    The store confirms

    The store contacts you to confirm the price, size, availability and delivery or pickup. No payment is taken in this demo form.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I order from Traffik?

Use the order form on this page, or send the style photo to the store’s Facebook inbox — the way the store already takes orders. The store confirms every order before it is final.

Is my order confirmed as soon as I send the form?

No. Sending the form is an enquiry only. Your order is confirmed only when the store contacts you to confirm the price, size, availability and delivery or pickup.

Are the prices on this page Traffik’s real prices?

No. All prices here are sample prices, to show how a catalogue can work. A public directory listing gave a men’s shirt at ৳700 as an example of the kind of item sold — but that is not a catalogue price on this page. The store confirms the real price when you order.

Do I need to pay when I order?

No payment is taken in this order form. Please ask the store directly about payment options when they confirm your order.

Can I visit a showroom instead of ordering online?

Yes — Traffik has four showrooms in Chattogram: Biponi Bitan (New Market), opposite New Market & GPO, Finlay Square and Sanmar Ocean City, Nasirabad. Opening hours were not verified, so please confirm them via the store’s Facebook Page before visiting.

How do I choose the right size?

Check the size guide on this page. If you are between two sizes, mention it in the order notes — the store can advise on the fit for the item you choose.

Do you deliver outside Chattogram?

Delivery was not verified for this concept demo. Please ask the store about delivery when they confirm your order, or pick up from one of the four Chattogram showrooms.
